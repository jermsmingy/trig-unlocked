<section id="identities" data-title="Identities">
  <div class="wrap">
    <div class="sec-head"><div class="sec-num">6</div><h2>The fundamental identities</h2></div>
    <p class="lead">An <b>identity</b> is an equation that's true for <i>every</i> value of the variable where both sides are defined. These are the tools you'll use to simplify and prove things.</p>
    <div class="split">
      <div class="card">
        <h3>The Pythagorean identity, seen</h3>
        <svg id="pySvg" class="viz" viewBox="-1.35 -1.35 2.7 2.7" aria-label="Unit circle triangle showing sin squared plus cos squared equals one"></svg>
        <input type="range" id="pyS" min="0" max="360" step="1" value="35" aria-label="Angle">
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <p>The triangle inside the unit circle has legs <span class="m c-cos">cos θ</span> and <span class="m c-sin">sin θ</span>, and hypotenuse <b>1</b> (the radius). Pythagoras says:</p>
        <div class="eq"><span class="m"><span class="c-sin">sin<sup>2</sup>θ</span> + <span class="c-cos">cos<sup>2</sup>θ</span> = 1</span></div>
        <div class="bar"><div id="pyBarS" style="background:var(--sin)"></div><div id="pyBarC" style="background:var(--cos)"></div></div>
        <p class="center m" id="pyNums"></p>
        <p>Divide every term by <span class="m">cos<sup>2</sup>θ</span> or by <span class="m">sin<sup>2</sup>θ</span> and you get the other two:</p>
        <div class="formula"><span class="m">\f{sin<sup>2</sup>θ}{cos<sup>2</sup>θ} + \f{cos<sup>2</sup>θ}{cos<sup>2</sup>θ} = \f{1}{cos<sup>2</sup>θ} @@E:nbsp@@⟹@@E:nbsp@@ <b>tan<sup>2</sup>θ + 1 = sec<sup>2</sup>θ</b></span></div>
        <div class="formula"><span class="m">\f{sin<sup>2</sup>θ}{sin<sup>2</sup>θ} + \f{cos<sup>2</sup>θ}{sin<sup>2</sup>θ} = \f{1}{sin<sup>2</sup>θ} @@E:nbsp@@⟹@@E:nbsp@@ <b>1 + cot<sup>2</sup>θ = csc<sup>2</sup>θ</b></span></div>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="grid2">
      <div class="card"><h3>Reciprocal</h3>
        <div class="formula"><span class="m">csc θ = \f{1}{sin θ} @@E:nbsp@@ sec θ = \f{1}{cos θ} @@E:nbsp@@ cot θ = \f{1}{tan θ}</span></div>
        <h3 style="margin-top:14px">Quotient</h3>
        <div class="formula"><span class="m">tan θ = \f{sin θ}{cos θ} @@E:nbsp@@@@E:nbsp@@ cot θ = \f{cos θ}{sin θ}</span></div></div>
      <div class="card"><h3>Even / odd</h3>
        <div class="formula"><span class="m">cos(−θ) = cos θ @@E:nbsp@@ sec(−θ) = sec θ</span> <span class="pill">even</span></div>
        <div class="formula"><span class="m">sin(−θ) = −sin θ @@E:nbsp@@ tan(−θ) = −tan θ</span> <span class="pill">odd</span></div>
        <div class="formula"><span class="m">csc(−θ) = −csc θ @@E:nbsp@@ cot(−θ) = −cot θ</span> <span class="pill">odd</span></div>
        <p class="small muted">Why: <span class="m">−θ</span> is the reflection of <span class="m">θ</span> across the <i>x</i>-axis. The <i>x</i>-coordinate (cos) stays the same; the <i>y</i>-coordinate (sin) flips sign.</p></div>
    </div>
    <div class="card"><h3>Cofunction ("co" = complement)</h3>
      <div class="grid3">
        <div class="formula"><span class="m">sin(\f{π}{2} − θ) = cos θ</span></div><div class="formula"><span class="m">tan(\f{π}{2} − θ) = cot θ</span></div><div class="formula"><span class="m">sec(\f{π}{2} − θ) = csc θ</span></div>
        <div class="formula"><span class="m">cos(\f{π}{2} − θ) = sin θ</span></div><div class="formula"><span class="m">cot(\f{π}{2} − θ) = tan θ</span></div><div class="formula"><span class="m">csc(\f{π}{2} − θ) = sec θ</span></div>
      </div>
      <p class="small muted">In a right triangle, the two acute angles add to 90°, and the side that's "opposite" for one angle is "adjacent" for the other. So sin of one angle = cos of its complement. That's where the "co" in cosine comes from.</p></div>

    <div class="card" id="checker">
      <h3>🔬 Identity checker: graph both sides</h3>
      <p>Pick an identity, or <b>type your own</b>. If the two graphs land exactly on top of each other everywhere, it's (very likely) an identity. If they separate anywhere, it's not.</p>
      <label class="ctl" for="idPreset">Choose one</label>
      <select id="idPreset"></select>
      <div class="grid2" style="margin-top:8px">
        <div><label class="ctl" for="idL">Left side</label><input type="text" id="idL" class="mathin" autocomplete="off" spellcheck="false"><div class="preview" id="idLp"></div></div>
        <div><label class="ctl" for="idR">Right side</label><input type="text" id="idR" class="mathin" autocomplete="off" spellcheck="false"><div class="preview" id="idRp"></div></div>
      </div>
      <canvas id="idCanvas" class="plot" aria-label="Graph comparing both sides of an identity"></canvas>
      <div class="legend"><span style="color:var(--accent)">left side</span><span style="color:var(--tan)">right side (dashed)</span></div>
      <div id="idVerdict" class="tip"></div>
      <details class="small muted"><summary>How to type expressions</summary>
        <p>Use <code>x</code> (or θ) as the variable. Functions: <code>sin cos tan csc sec cot sqrt abs</code>. Powers: <code>sin^2 x</code> or <code>(sin x)^2</code>.     <div class="grid2">
      <div class="card">
        <h3>Sum @@E:amp@@ difference</h3>
        <div class="formula"><span class="m">sin(α ± β) = sin α cos β ± cos α sin β</span></div>
        <div class="formula"><span class="m">cos(α ± β) = cos α cos β ∓ sin α sin β</span></div>
        <div class="formula"><span class="m">tan(α ± β) = \f{tan α ± tan β}{1 ∓ tan α tan β}</span></div>
        <p class="small muted">Note the cosine formula <b>flips</b> the sign (∓).</p>
